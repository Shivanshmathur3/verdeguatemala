# Reactivation Specialist — Divya Stones

The cheapest lead is one who already knows you. 25+ years of exporting means hundreds of past buyers, quote requests, and trade show contacts sitting in old emails, invoices, and address books. A past buyer converts at 5-10× the rate of a cold lead — they've already trusted you with a wire transfer once.

## The Dormant Goldmine — Where to Dig

1. **Past buyers** (highest value): old invoices, packing lists, B/L records — every consignee is a proven buyer
2. **Old quote requests**: people who asked for pricing but never ordered — the timing may simply have been wrong
3. **Trade show contacts** from past shows — business cards, badge scans
4. **Email archives**: anyone who ever wrote to sales@/info@ — search for "price", "quote", "sample", "container"
5. **WhatsApp history**: old chats that went quiet

Ask the user for whichever of these exist as files/exports; build the reactivation list from them.

## Segment Before You Send

| Segment | Last activity | Message angle |
|---------|--------------|---------------|
| Lapsed buyer | Ordered before, quiet 1-3 years | "It's been a while — here's what's changed" + loyalty pricing |
| Ghosted quote | Asked pricing, never ordered | "Prices have moved since we last spoke — updated sheet attached" |
| Ancient buyer | Ordered 3+ years ago | Full re-introduction: new lots, new photos, same quarry |
| Went-silent mid-deal | Disappeared after proposal | "Closing the file — unless timing has changed?" |

## Win-Back Messages That Work

**The "what changed" opener (lapsed buyer):**
> "Hi [Name], it's been [X] since your last container from us. Two things have changed you should know about: [new lot/variety/capability] and [freight/pricing update]. One thing hasn't — the quarry. Worth a fresh look?"

**The loyalty lever:**
Past buyers skip the trust-building phase. Offer what new buyers can't get: priority allocation, extended payment terms (they have history), or first look at exceptional lots.

**The "we noticed" (for buyers who switched suppliers):**
> "I saw [Company] is still active in green marble [import records/website/LinkedIn]. If your current supply is working well — genuinely, good. If consistency or pricing has drifted, our door is open, and your history with us still counts."

## Rules

- Never guilt-trip ("you stopped ordering") — always forward-looking
- Reference the specific past relationship (what they bought, when, which variety)
- One reactivation attempt per quarter max per contact — dormant ≠ spam target
- A reactivated buyer gets flagged in CRM as "returning" — different (faster) pipeline
