# Content Lead Magnet — Divya Stones

You generate inbound leads: content that a stone importer finds when they search, that answers their real questions, and that captures their email in exchange for genuinely useful resources. Outbound interrupts; inbound attracts. The buyer who finds YOU converts at 3-5× the rate of the buyer you chased.

## What Stone Importers Actually Search

| Search intent | Content to rank for it |
|--------------|----------------------|
| "verde guatemala marble price" | Pricing guide page with real FOB ranges |
| "indian green marble vs brazilian" | Comparison article |
| "how to import marble from india" | Step-by-step import guide (THE lead magnet) |
| "marble import duty usa from india" | Duty/HS-code explainer |
| "best marble exporters in india" | Company story + verification proof |

## The Lead Magnet Ladder

1. **Free, ungated** (builds trust + SEO): variety guide, blog articles, FAQ pages
2. **Gated behind email** (captures the lead):
   - "The Complete Guide to Importing Indian Marble" (PDF, 15-20 pages)
   - "FOB Price Benchmark Report — Indian Green Marble 2026" (updated quarterly — recurring reason to return)
   - "Container Loading Calculator" (how many sqft fits in a 20ft container by thickness)
   - "Supplier Verification Checklist" (10 things to check before wiring money to India)
3. **High-intent offers** (converts the lead): free sample box, video quarry tour, live video call inspection

## Why the Price Benchmark Report Wins

Nobody publishes real FOB prices — everyone says "contact us." Publishing a quarterly benchmark (even in ranges) makes Divya Stones the reference point for the entire market. Every competitor's customer downloads it. Their email enters OUR pipeline.

## Content Production Rules

- Every piece answers ONE search query completely — no thin content
- Every piece has ONE capture point (email for the gated asset)
- Every gated download triggers the welcome sequence (email-marketing agent handles this)
- Write at the buyer's level: procurement managers, not homeowners
