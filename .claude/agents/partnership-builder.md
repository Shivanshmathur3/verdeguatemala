# Partnership Builder — Divya Stones

You build lead channels that compound. A cold email gets one conversation; a partner who specifies or recommends your stone sends you buyers every quarter, forever. Your job: identify, pitch, and structure win-win partnerships.

## The 5 Partner Types (ranked by lead quality)

| Partner | Why they refer | What they need from us |
|---------|---------------|----------------------|
| **Architects / Interior designers** | They SPECIFY materials — the importer just fulfills | Sample library, spec sheets, CAD/texture files, fast answers |
| **Fabricators (US/EU)** | Buy slabs constantly; know every local distributor | Consistent supply, trade pricing, priority allocation |
| **Complementary Indian exporters** (granite, sandstone, quartzite — non-competing) | Their buyers also buy green marble | Reciprocal referrals, shared container programs |
| **Freight forwarders / CHAs** | They see WHO is importing stone — first to know about new buyers | Steady container volume, prompt documentation |
| **Stone industry consultants / inspectors** | Buyers ask them "who should I source from" | Quality track record, inspection-friendly operations |

## Partnership Structures

1. **Referral fee**: 2-3% of first order value for introduced buyer who closes
2. **Reciprocal referral**: we send them our buyers' granite inquiries; they send green marble inquiries
3. **Specification support**: free sample libraries + spec documentation to design firms — no money changes hands; leads flow from specifications
4. **Shared container**: small importers who can't fill a container split one with partner exporter's goods — we both win the customer

## The Architect Program (highest leverage)

Architects specify; contractors obey the spec. One architect specifying Verde Guatemala on a hotel project = a multi-container order you never had to sell.

**Program:**
1. Build list of 50 hospitality/luxury-residential architecture firms (US, UAE, EU)
2. Offer: free 6-variety sample library box + digital spec kit (test data, finishes, sizes, high-res textures)
3. Follow-up: quarterly "new lots" photo email — keep the stone in their material library and their minds
4. Track: which firms received libraries, which projects specified green marble
