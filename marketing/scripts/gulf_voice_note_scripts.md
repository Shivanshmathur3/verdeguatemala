## Why a voice note

Gulf business runs on relationships and voice. A 30-second voice note after two text touches puts a real person behind the number — it is the WhatsApp equivalent of the plan's factory video: proof of life. Nobody scam-operates a warm, unhurried voice note.

---

## Core Script — Fit-Out / Interiors Contractor (30 seconds, word for word)

> "Salam [First Name], this is [Your Name] from Divya Stones in Udaipur, India. I sent you our catalog and some slab photos this week — I just wanted to put a voice behind the messages. We're quarry owners. We've been exporting granite and marble since 2000, and we ship to Jeddah and Jebel Ali in ten to fourteen days. If you have a package coming up that needs stone, send me the spec right here — I'll have a price back to you within two days. Thanks, [First Name]. Talk soon."

*(~95 words ≈ 30–32 seconds at a natural, unhurried pace.)*

We're quarry owners, exporting since 2000, and we do a lot of mixed containers for traders — granite, marble, quartz, one box, ready for re-export to Saudi, Oman, or Africa. If that fits your business, message me one word — 'prices' — and I'll send the trader list today. Thanks, [First Name]."

*(~85 words ≈ 28–30 seconds. The one-word CTA is deliberate — traders are busy and it works.)*

> "Salam [First Name], this is [Your Name] from Divya Stones in India. I shared our catalog and some material photos for [PROJECT_REFERENCE] earlier this week. I know submittals are the real work on packages like yours — so along with pricing, we provide full technical data sheets, batch consistency records, and physical samples couriered within forty-eight hours. *(~85 words ≈ 28–30 seconds. Speaks to submittal pain, not product features. Never claim vendor-list status on NEOM, Red Sea, Diriyah, or ROSHN — reference their package, not our involvement. See the credibility rules in the sequence file.)*

## Delivery Rules

1. **One take, quiet room.** A small stumble is human and fine; background noise and re-recorded stiffness are not.
2. **30 seconds hard target, 35 max.** WhatsApp shows the duration before they press play — over 45 seconds often never gets played.
3. **Smile while recording.** It is audible.
4. **Speak, don't read.** Rehearse twice, then record without looking at the script. If it sounds read, re-record.
5. **Say their name twice** — open and close. It is the strongest personalization a voice carries.
6. **One CTA only,** matching the segment: send the spec / say "prices" / send the BOQ.
7. **Never open a sequence with a voice note.** It belongs at Touch 3, after the catalog and photos have earned the familiarity.
8. **If they replied earlier in the sequence, do not send this cold script.** Record a fresh personal one referencing what they said.
9. **Follow with the factory video 1–2 minutes later** — voice note first, video second, per Touch 3 structure.

## Arabic Greeting in Voice Notes

- Opening with **"As-salamu alaykum, [First Name]"** is warm and appropriate when the contact is a Saudi/Emirati national or came via an Arab referral — then continue naturally in English: "…this is [Your Name] from Divya Stones…"
- For expat-run Dubai trading companies and fit-out teams (very often Indian, Pakistani, Filipino, or Lebanese management), stay with "Salam [First Name]" or "Hi [First Name]" — safer and still warm.
- **Never record a full Arabic voice note unless the speaker genuinely speaks Arabic.** A rehearsed phonetic sentence followed by English is instantly recognizable and costs credibility. Greeting only.
- If they reply in Arabic, hand the thread to an Arabic speaker or say honestly: "My Arabic is limited — English okay?"
