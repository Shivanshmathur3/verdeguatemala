# Segment A — Still in the Market, Not Buying From India

**Who:** Past buyers still importing stone, but sourcing from Brazil / Turkey / China / elsewhere.
**Goal 1:** Learn WHY they left India-sourcing — this is free market research that shapes all cold messaging.
**Goal 2:** Pitch the post-tariff math (US) or new categories (quartz, porcelain, veneer).
**Channel order:** personal email from the owner → WhatsApp → call. NO automation — these people know us.

---

## Discovery Questions (use verbatim on the call — in this order)

1. "What are you mainly buying these days, and from where?"
2. "When you moved away from Indian suppliers — what drove it? Price, freight, quality consistency, payment terms, or communication?"
3. "What would an Indian supplier have to prove for you to give them a container again?"
4. "What are you paying landed right now for [their main material]? Roughly."
5. "If I showed you post-tariff landed math on the same material — worth a look, or is the relationship with your current supplier untouchable?"

**Log every answer in `reactivation_tracker.csv` — the `intelligence_learned` column is the point of this segment.**

## Then-Pitch Rules
- If price was the issue → post-tariff landed-cost comparison (US) or direct-quarry math
- If quality/consistency was the issue → Day 9 consistency proof pack + facility video + inspection-welcome offer
- If payment terms → 30% advance / 70% against BL copy; extended terms for a proven past buyer
- If they buy categories we now carry (quartz, porcelain, veneer) → new-categories one-liner
- **Always end with the referral ask:** "Who else in your market should I be talking to?"
