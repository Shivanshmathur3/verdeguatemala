# Segment B — Exited the Market

**Who:** Past buyers who retired, sold the business, or left stone importing.
**Goal:** Introductions. One introduction from a retired importer beats 50 cold emails. They know exactly who took their customers and who is buying Indian stone in their market now.
**Channel:** warm call first (these are relationship calls); email only if unreachable by phone.

---

## The Warm Call Script

**Opening (warm, unhurried):**
> "[FIRST_NAME], this is [OWNER_NAME] from Divya Stones in Udaipur — we shipped you [MATERIALS] back in [YEARS]. How have you been? ... I heard you [retired / sold / moved on] — how's that treating you?"

**The honest frame:**
> "I'll tell you why I'm calling. I'm rebuilding our export book this year, and you knew your market better than anyone I ever sold to. I'm not calling to sell you anything — I'm calling for your Rolodex."

**The three asks (in order, verbatim):**
1. "Who took over your customers when you stepped back?"
2. "Who's buying Indian stone in your market now — who are the names I should know?"
3. "Would you be comfortable introducing me to one or two of them? Even a one-line email or a 'mention my name' helps more than you'd think."

**If they hesitate:**
> "No pressure at all. If it's easier, I'll draft a two-line introduction email and you can just forward it if you're comfortable."

**Close (every call, no exception):**
> "Thank you — genuinely. And if you're ever in Udaipur, the quarry visit and dinner are on me. That's a standing offer."

**After the call:** same-day thank-you WhatsApp. If they promised an introduction, send them the ready-to-forward blurb within the hour (below).

## Ready-to-Forward Introduction Blurb (send it to THEM to forward)

```
Subject: Intro — Divya Stones (Indian stone exporter I worked with)

[NEW_CONTACT_NAME] — connecting you with [OWNER_NAME] of Divya Stones,
Udaipur. I imported [MATERIALS] from them for [X] years — quality and
documentation were never a problem. With the US tariff gone they're
worth a fresh look. I'll let you two take it from here.
```
