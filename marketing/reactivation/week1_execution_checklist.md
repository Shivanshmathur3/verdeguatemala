# Priority 0 — Week 1 Execution Checklist
**The plan says this is Week 1's most important job — more important than any tool purchase.**

## Days 1–3: Dig (consolidate every record into the master list)

- [ ] **Old email accounts**: search sales@/info@/owner inboxes for: "purchase order", "proforma", "B/L", "bill of lading", "container", "shipment", "LC", "advance" — export sender company + contact + date
- [ ] **Invoice files / accounting records**: every export invoice since 2000 — company, country, material, year
- [ ] **Bills of Lading and packing lists**: consignee names = confirmed buyers (the gold source)
- [ ] **Old phone / WhatsApp**: scroll contacts + chats for buyer numbers that went quiet
- [ ] **Physical records**: business cards, trade show notebooks, old diaries
- [ ] **Customs data cross-check** (once Volza/ImportGenius is purchased): search "Divya" as shipper — your own shipment history lists your own buyers

## Days 4–5: Consolidate & Segment

- [ ] Merge everything into `master_list_template.csv` — one row per company, dedup by company name
- [ ] Fill `status` via 10-minute web check per company: website live? still in stone? (LinkedIn/Google)
- [ ] Assign segment: **A** = active in market, not buying from India · **B** = exited · **C** = still occasionally buying from us
- [ ] Rank within each segment by `est_lifetime_volume` — biggest past relationships first

## Days 6–7: First Sends (owner-voice, top 10 per segment)

- [ ] Segment C first (fastest wins): email + price list to every C contact
- [ ] Segment A: top 10 owner emails (template in `segment_A_still_in_market.md`)
- [ ] Segment B: schedule top 5 warm calls for Week 2 (calls need the right hour, not a rush)
- [ ] Log every send in `reactivation_tracker.csv`

## Success Criteria (from the plan)
3–8 warm introductions · a clear map of why buyers left India-sourcing (fuels cold messaging) · a handful of reactivated small accounts.

**Rule:** NO automation on this list. Personal email from the owner, then WhatsApp, then a call.
