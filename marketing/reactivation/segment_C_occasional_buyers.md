# Segment C — Still Occasionally Buying

**Who:** Past buyers who still place orders now and then — the smallest segment, the highest conversion.
**Goal:** Win-back to regular cadence with current pricing + new categories.
**Channel:** owner email → WhatsApp. These convert on speed and ease, not persuasion.

---

## Owner Email

```
Subject: [FIRST_NAME] — updated price list + what's new at the quarry

Hi [FIRST_NAME],

[OWNER_NAME] from Divya Stones. Your last container was [LAST_ORDER —
material, year]. Since then, three things changed that matter to you:

1. US tariff repeal — landed cost on Indian stone is down 30%+ from
   what you paid in 2025 [US buyers only]
2. Current price list attached — [MATERIALS_THEY_BUY] pricing has
   moved in your favor
3. We now also supply quartz, porcelain, and stone veneer — if you're
   buying those elsewhere, one consolidated container beats two suppliers

As a past account you skip the queue: priority allocation and your old
payment terms still stand.

Want me to hold space on the next vessel? One reply does it.

[OWNER_NAME]
Divya Stones | +91-94141-67278
```

## WhatsApp Version (2–3 days later if no reply)

```
[FIRST_NAME], [OWNER_NAME] from Divya Stones 🇮🇳 — sent you our updated
price list by email. Short version: [MATERIALS] pricing moved in your
favor, and we now do quartz/porcelain/veneer too. Photos of current
lots: [ATTACH 2-3]. Shall I hold space on the next vessel for you?
```

## Rules
- Attach the actual price list — no "reply to receive pricing" friction with a known buyer
- Reference their exact last order — proves this isn't a blast
- One concrete close: "hold space on the next vessel"
- If they order: flag as `returning` in CRM — faster pipeline, skip qualification
- If no response after email + WhatsApp + one call: quarterly nurture only, don't burn the relationship
