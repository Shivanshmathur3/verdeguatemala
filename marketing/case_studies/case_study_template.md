## Placeholder Case Study (for use until real case studies are populated)

**Stone Distributor in Dallas, TX — Reduced landed cost by 28%, consistent supply across 3 containers**

**The situation:** A Dallas-based stone distributor was sourcing Verde Guatemala through a New York broker and facing two problems: margin compression from the broker tier, and colour inconsistency between containers that was generating complaints from their own customers.

**What they needed:** Direct quarry access, consistent Forest Green grading, and pre-shipment photo approval before each container was loaded.

**What Divya Stones delivered:** Three containers over 8 months, all shipped within the agreed 6-week lead time, with full pre-shipment photo documentation. The buyer's landed cost dropped 28% compared to their broker pricing on equivalent grades.

**What they said:** "Consistent quality, reliable lead times, and they photograph everything before it ships. That last part matters more than people realise — we stopped having disputes with our customers about what we delivered."

**Products supplied:**
- Variety: Forest Green, Verde Guatemala
- Finish: Polished
- Format: 260x60 cm slabs, 2 cm
- Volume: 3 containers over 8 months

## Notes on collecting real case studies
- Ask for a quote after the second successful shipment — not the first (trust is higher)
- Email template: "We'd love to share your experience with other buyers considering Divya Stones. Would you be comfortable with a one-sentence quote we could use on our website and in introductions to new customers? No pressure at all."
- Keep quotes short (1-2 sentences) — long testimonials are skimmed or ignored
- Always get written permission before using a customer's name or company name externally
