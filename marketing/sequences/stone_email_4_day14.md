## Personalization Notes
- This email works best when sent to contacts who have opened at least one prior email but not replied
- If the contact has already replied and is in conversation, skip this email — the sample offer is more powerful when made verbally on a call
- Tailor the variety list to what you know of their preferences if earlier emails generated engagement signals

## Fulfillment Checklist (triggered when address is received)
- [ ] Pack 6 x 4x4" polished samples in foam-lined box
- [ ] Include Divya Stones business card + one-page variety guide
- [ ] Ship DHL Express with tracking; email tracking number same day
- [ ] Follow up 5 days post-delivery to ask for feedback

## CTA
Single ask: share a shipping address.

## Tone
Generous, low-pressure. The offer removes the risk of engaging — make it feel effortless for them to say yes.
