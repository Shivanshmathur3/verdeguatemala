## ⏱️ THE 2-HOUR SLA — READ FIRST

> **Every reply and every inbound enquiry gets a response within 2 hours during Gulf business hours: Sunday–Thursday, 08:00–18:00 GST (UTC+4).**
>
> - GST is 1.5 hours BEHIND India (IST). 08:00 GST = 09:30 IST, 18:00 GST = 19:30 IST. Staff the phone accordingly.
> - Reply arrives outside those hours (Gulf evening, Friday, Saturday) → respond by **09:00 GST the next Gulf business day**.
> - Gulf buyers treat WhatsApp like a phone call, not email. A 6-hour silence reads as "not serious."
> - **If the 2-hour SLA breaks in any week, cut send volume in half until it holds** (mirrors plan Hard Truth #4: aggressive outbound with slow follow-up is wasted money).

## Cadence & Timing

| Touch | Day | Content | Send window (GST) |
|-------|-----|---------|-------------------|
| 1 | Day 0 | Intro + catalog PDF | Sun–Thu, 09:30–11:30 |
| 2 | Day 3 | Project-relevant slab photos | Sun–Thu, 09:30–11:30 |
| 3 | Day 7 | Voice note + 90-sec factory video | Sun–Thu, 16:00–18:00 |
| 4 | Day 12 | Call ask, two proposed slots | Sun–Thu, 09:30–11:30 |

**Timing rules:**
- Never send Friday or Saturday (Gulf weekend). If a touch lands on Fri/Sat, push to Sunday.
- Never send after 19:00 or before 08:30 GST.
- During Ramadan: shift sends to 10:00–13:00 GST, expect slower replies, extend gaps by 2–3 days, do not chase.
- **Any reply at any touch = exit the sequence immediately.** A human takes over, 2-hour SLA applies, quote/samples within 48h (plan KPI).
- No reply after Touch 4 → mark dormant in CRM, recycle with fresh material photos in 90 days.

**Style rules (all touches):**
- Under 120 words per message — these run 50–85. Shorter always wins on WhatsApp.
- WhatsApp-natural: short lines, no email formality, no "I hope this message finds you well."
- Emoji: maximum one per message, none in Touch 1. Attachments do the visual work.
- Product frame is WIDE per plan: lead with what THEY buy — granite (Steel Grey, Black Galaxy), green marble, quartz, porcelain — not Verde Guatemala only.

## Touch 2 — Day 3 · Project-Relevant Material Photos

**Attach:** 3–4 full-slab photos of ACTUAL current lots matching their building type or project. Real slabs only — Gulf buyers spot library images instantly.

```
Hi [First Name], a few current lots that fit [PROJECT_REFERENCE] 📸

[ATTACH: 3–4 FULL-SLAB PHOTOS — material name + thickness + finish
captioned on each]

These are live slabs in our yard, not renders — I can send a lot video
for anything that fits your spec.

Want FOB or CIF [Jebel Ali / Jeddah / Dammam] pricing on any of these?
```
*(~55 words. One CTA: ask for pricing.)*

**[PROJECT_REFERENCE] token — fill from research, one line, specific:**
e.g. "the hotel fit-outs you're running in Riyadh" / "Diriyah-area hospitality packages" / "your villa cladding projects in Sharjah" / "the ROSHN community packages now in tender."

### Giga-Project Reference Hooks — use ONE, credibly, never overclaim

| Project | The one-line hook | Credibility rule |
|---------|-------------------|------------------|
| **NEOM / Red Sea** | "If your NEOM or Red Sea package specs call for documented sourcing and tight tolerances, we can supply full technical data sheets and batch traceability with every lot." | We reference their PUBLISHED specs and our ability to meet them — never claim to be an approved/registered NEOM vendor or past supplier. |
| **Diriyah Gate** | "For Diriyah-style Najdi work — natural stone, honed and sandblasted finishes — we can match that palette from current Indian granite and marble lots." | We speak to the architectural style and finishes, not to any involvement in the project itself. |
| **ROSHN** | "For ROSHN-scale residential volume, our value is batch consistency — the same shade and finish across thousands of units, at a price point that holds." | We pitch repeatability at volume, the real procurement pain — never imply we're on a ROSHN vendor list. |

**Golden rule:** reference the project as THEIR context, never as OUR track record. "If your package needs X, we can do X" — never "we supply NEOM."

## Arabic-Greeting Variant — When and When Not

**Use "As-salamu alaykum [First Name]," as the Touch 1 opener when:**
- The contact is a Saudi or Emirati national (Arabic name + KSA/UAE profile), or
- They came via referral from an Arab business contact, or
- Their own messages/WhatsApp status are in Arabic.

**Do NOT use it when:**
- The contact is Gulf-based expat management — many Dubai trading companies and fit-out procurement teams are run by Indian, Pakistani, Filipino, or Lebanese professionals. "Hi [First Name]" or "Salam [First Name]" is the safe default there.
- You cannot continue in Arabic. Opening in Arabic and instantly switching to English reads as a template, which defeats the purpose. **Greeting only — never a full Arabic message unless someone on the team can genuinely hold the conversation.**
- If they reply "Wa alaykum as-salam" and continue in English, continue in English. If they continue in Arabic, hand the thread to an Arabic speaker or say honestly: "My Arabic is limited — English okay?" Honesty beats Google Translate every time.
