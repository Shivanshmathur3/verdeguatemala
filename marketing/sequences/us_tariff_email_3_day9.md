# US Post-Tariff Sequence — Email 3 (Day 9: Consistency Proof)

**Sequence:** US cold email, 5 touches / 21 days (MASTER_ACQUISITION_PLAN.md, Section 2)
**Send day:** Day 9
**Goal:** Kill the #1 fear about switching back to an Indian supplier — batch-to-batch consistency. Show packing standard, tolerance, resin treatment, loading proof.
**Single CTA:** Reply for the one-page packing spec (bundled with their quote).

---

## Subject

```
Will container 5 match container 1? How we make sure.
```


## Operator notes

- **Photo placeholders:** use real container-loading photos from recent shipments (proof assets, Plan Section 5). Never stock imagery — customs-savvy buyers cross-check.
- **Packing spec PDF** lives in `marketing/proof_assets/` (export credentials PDF includes packing spec per Plan Section 5, item 4). Send only on reply — keeps this email attachment-free for deliverability.
- **Cold-call trigger:** prospects who have opened 2x with no reply by Day 9–10 enter the cold-call queue (Plan Section 2: 10 calls/day; script at `marketing/scripts/us_cold_call_script.md`).
- Adjust the bullet list if a material's process differs (e.g., granite lots that skip resin) — never claim a treatment a lot didn't get.
