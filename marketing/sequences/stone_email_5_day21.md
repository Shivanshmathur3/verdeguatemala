## Personalization Notes
- Keep this email exactly as short as written — do not add more content
- The brevity itself signals respect for their time and often triggers a reply from contacts who were watching but not ready
- Do NOT include a hard CTA or offer in this email; the open door is the CTA
- Tag this contact in your CRM as "Sequence Complete — Re-engage Q[next quarter]" and set a 90-day reminder

## Post-Sequence Actions
- Move contact to a low-frequency nurture list (1 email per quarter: new lot updates, price movements, trade show appearances)
- If they open this final email, flag them as a warm contact and reach out personally within 48 hours

## Tone
Warm, dignified, zero pressure. This email should feel like a handshake on the way out — not a last-ditch sales attempt.
