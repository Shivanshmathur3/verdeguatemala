## Personalization Notes
- If Email 1 generated an open but no reply, do not reference the previous email — let this one stand on its own as a visual value-drop
- Tailor the photo selection to the contact's apparent market segment (high-end boutique vs. mass wholesale vs. online retail)
- Seasonal note: for Q4 outreach, lead with gift-season pieces; for Q1-Q2, lead with everyday wear and bridal

## Attachment Checklist
- [ ] Photo 1: Statement necklace — gemstone close-up visible
- [ ] Photo 2: Ring set — lifestyle or flat-lay shot
- [ ] Photo 3: Earring pair — clean product shot, white background
- Keep images under 3 MB each; use a shareable Google Drive folder link as fallback

## CTA
Single ask: reply if any pieces fit their buyers, to receive pricing and specs.

## Tone
Visual, minimal copy. The photos carry this email — keep the words out of the way.
