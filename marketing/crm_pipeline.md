## SECTION 1 — PIPELINE STAGES

### Overview

The pipeline has 10 stages. Every lead must pass through stages sequentially. No lead may skip stages. A lead is only moved forward when the exit criteria for the current stage are fully met — not when an action is taken, but when a verifiable outcome is achieved.

Stage ownership: the sales rep assigned to the lead owns every stage transition and is accountable for SLA compliance.

---

### Stage 1 — Lead

**Definition:** A company or individual has been identified as a potential buyer of Verde Guatemala marble. Contact information exists. No outreach has occurred yet.

**Entry Criteria:**
- Contact name and company name are recorded in CRM
- At minimum one verified contact channel exists (email OR LinkedIn OR WhatsApp)
- Source of lead is documented (e.g., trade directory, ImportYeti, LinkedIn search, referral, inbound form)
- Country and segment are tagged (Stone Distributor / Jewelry Wholesaler / EU Buyer / Architect-Designer / Other)

**Exit Criteria:**
- First outreach message has been sent (email, LinkedIn DM, or WhatsApp)
- Message content and send timestamp are logged in CRM
- Follow-up reminder is set in CRM

**SLA:** Lead must receive first outreach within 24 hours of being added to the CRM. Leads added on Friday must be contacted by Monday 9:00 AM.

**SLA Status Rules:**
- On Track: outreach sent within 24 hours
- At Risk: 18–24 hours elapsed, no outreach sent
- Overdue: 24+ hours elapsed, no outreach sent

**Stall Action:** If overdue, sales rep receives automated CRM alert. Manager reviews if stall exceeds 48 hours.

---

### Stage 2 — Contacted

**Definition:** First outreach has been sent. We are waiting for a response. The lead is aware of Divya Stones (or will be once they open the message).

**Entry Criteria:**
- First outreach message logged and timestamped
- Message type recorded (cold email / LinkedIn DM / WhatsApp / referral intro)
- Lead moved from Stage 1 with exit criteria confirmed

**Exit Criteria:**
- Lead has replied to any message (email, DM, WhatsApp), OR
- A phone/video call has been completed with the lead, OR
- Lead has clicked a link or downloaded an attachment (if tracked)

**SLA:** Response or meaningful interaction must occur within 48 hours of first contact. If no response, a second touchpoint (different channel or different angle) must be sent at the 48-hour mark.

**SLA Status Rules:**
- On Track: < 48 hours since first outreach, no response yet
- At Risk: 36–48 hours elapsed with no response
- Overdue: 48+ hours elapsed, no response, no second touchpoint sent

**Stall Action:** At Day 2 (48 hours), trigger second email using alternate subject line and value angle. At Day 7, trigger third touchpoint (WhatsApp or LinkedIn voice note). At Day 14 with no response, move to Nurture and log reason.

---

### Stage 3 — MQL (Marketing Qualified Lead)

**Definition:** The lead has shown genuine interest — they responded, clicked, asked a question, or engaged in a way that signals real curiosity about sourcing Verde Guatemala. They are not yet sales-ready but warrant active follow-up.

**Entry Criteria:**
- A response has been received (even a short "send me more info" qualifies)
- OR: the lead has clicked a product link, downloaded a spec sheet, or viewed a shared portfolio link (if tracked)
- Lead is NOT yet disqualified (no clear signal they are the wrong segment, wrong budget, or irrelevant)

**Exit Criteria:**
- A qualifying conversation has taken place (call or substantive email exchange) AND
- At least 2 of 4 BANT criteria have been partially confirmed (see BANT scorecard in Section 2)
- BANT partial score of 12 or above (out of 40)

**SLA:** MQL must receive a personalised response within 4 hours of their reply arriving. A discovery call must be proposed within 72 hours. If no call is booked within 72 hours, escalate to manager.

**SLA Status Rules:**
- On Track: response sent within 4 hours, call proposed within 72 hours
- At Risk: response sent, but 48–72 hours elapsed with no call scheduled
- Overdue: 72+ hours elapsed, no call scheduled

**Stall Action:** If the lead goes quiet after initial interest, send a concrete value hook ("Our Verde Guatemala slab sizes are X — most distributors in your region order Y, let me show you the comparison"). If still no response at Day 7, move to Nurture.

---

### Stage 4 — SQL (Sales Qualified Lead)

**Definition:** The lead has been confirmed as a viable buying opportunity. They have the budget, authority, a real need, and a workable timeline. This is a sales conversation, not a marketing conversation.

**Entry Criteria:**
- BANT full score of 20 or above (out of 40)
- Budget: lead has indicated an annual stone/marble spend or container volume that justifies Divya Stones' minimum order
- Authority: confirmed that the contact can approve or directly influence purchasing decisions
- Need: Verde Guatemala marble (or equivalent green marble) is either actively sought or currently sourced from a competitor
- Timeline: sourcing decision is expected within the next 6 months or sooner

**Exit Criteria:**
- A discovery/qualification call has been completed
- Call notes are logged in CRM (summary of need, volumes, timeline, competitors, concerns)
- Next step agreed upon and documented with the lead

**SLA:** SQL must receive a formal follow-up email within 2 hours of the qualifying call ending. A discovery call must be completed within 7 days of reaching SQL status.

**SLA Status Rules:**
- On Track: call completed, follow-up sent within 2 hours
- At Risk: call completed, no follow-up sent 2–6 hours later
- Overdue: 6+ hours since call, no follow-up sent; OR SQL status for 7+ days with no call completed

---

### Stage 5 — Discovery

**Definition:** A substantive call or meeting has occurred. We understand the lead's specific needs, volumes, quality standards, logistics requirements, and competitive alternatives. We are building a tailored proposal.

**Entry Criteria:**
- Discovery call completed and logged (date, duration, attendees, key notes)
- Lead's requirements captured:
  - Stone type and finish (e.g., Verde Guatemala polished slabs, tiles, blocks)
  - Volume per order or annual requirement
  - Delivery destination and preferred Incoterm
  - Quality standards and certifications required
  - Budget range or reference pricing they currently pay
  - Decision timeline and decision-making process
  - Current suppliers and why they are considering alternatives

**Exit Criteria:**
- Custom proposal or quote has been prepared (not sent yet — that is the next stage)
- Proposal has been reviewed internally before sending
- Lead has confirmed they are expecting the proposal and the send date has been agreed

**SLA:** Proposal must be prepared and sent within 7 days of the discovery call. If a complex quote requires more time, the lead must be notified proactively with a revised timeline.

**SLA Status Rules:**
- On Track: proposal in preparation, within 7-day window
- At Risk: Day 5–7, proposal not yet drafted
- Overdue: 7+ days since discovery call, proposal not sent

---

### Stage 6 — Proposal Sent

**Definition:** A formal, itemised pricing proposal or quotation has been sent to the lead. The lead is reviewing it.

**Entry Criteria:**
- Proposal document sent (email with PDF quote OR shared Google Doc)
- Proposal includes: product specifications, unit pricing (USD/sqm or USD/MT), MOQ, payment terms, lead time, sample availability, and Incoterm
- Send date and proposal version logged in CRM
- Proposal expiry date set (standard: 30 days from send date)

**Exit Criteria:**
- Lead has responded to the proposal (questions, counter-offer, or request to negotiate), OR
- Lead has verbally or in writing accepted the terms, OR
- Lead has rejected the proposal (move to Closed Lost or Nurture)

**SLA:** Follow up on the proposal within 24 hours of sending (a brief message confirming receipt and offering to answer questions). If no response within 5 days, send a structured follow-up. If no response within 10 days, escalate with a revised offer or additional value (sample offer, case study, reference).

**SLA Status Rules:**
- On Track: < 5 days since proposal sent, awaiting response
- At Risk: 5–10 days elapsed, no response to proposal
- Overdue: 10+ days, no response and no follow-up escalation taken

---

### Stage 7 — Negotiation

**Definition:** The lead is actively engaging on the terms. This may include price negotiation, volume adjustments, payment term requests, sample requirements, or legal/contract review.

**Entry Criteria:**
- Lead has responded to the proposal with questions, a counter-offer, or a request for adjustments
- Negotiation points are logged in CRM (what is being disputed or discussed)
- Internal pricing floor and non-negotiables are defined before engaging

**Exit Criteria:**
- Final terms agreed upon and confirmed in writing (email confirmation or signed pro-forma invoice), OR
- Lead has declined after negotiation (move to Closed Lost)
- If samples are required before final agreement, sample dispatch triggers a parallel Sample Sent sub-status

**SLA:** Respond to any negotiation message within 24 hours. Revised proposals or counter-offers must be returned within 48 hours. Negotiations must not run beyond 14 days without a defined next step or escalation.

**SLA Status Rules:**
- On Track: active exchange occurring, within 14-day window
- At Risk: 7–14 days of negotiation with no clear resolution path
- Overdue: 14+ days in negotiation with no next step defined

---

### Stage 8 — Closed Won

**Definition:** The deal is confirmed. A Purchase Order has been received, or an advance payment/deposit has been made. Divya Stones is committed to fulfilling the order.

**Entry Criteria:**
- Written Purchase Order received, OR
- Advance payment (minimum 30% deposit per standard terms) received
- Order details confirmed: product, quantity, finish, packing, shipping timeline, Incoterm, port of destination
- Production or inventory allocation initiated

**Exit Criteria:** N/A — this is a terminal stage. Post-sale follow-up tasks are created:
- Delivery confirmation (when shipment leaves Udaipur)
- Document handover (invoice, BL, COO, quality cert)
- Feedback request 30 days after delivery
- Re-order reminder 90 days after delivery (move to a new Lead in the next cycle)

**SLA:** Order acknowledgement sent to buyer within 4 hours of PO receipt. Production update provided within 7 days. Shipment confirmed with tracking within the agreed lead time.

---

### Stage 9 — Closed Lost

**Definition:** The lead has explicitly declined, chosen a competitor, or become permanently unresponsive after all follow-up sequences are exhausted.

**Entry Criteria:**
- Lead has explicitly rejected the proposal or stated they will not proceed, OR
- Lead has been in Contacted or later stages for 30+ days with zero response to all touchpoints (email, WhatsApp, LinkedIn, phone)
- Loss reason is documented (required — must not be left blank)

**Exit Criteria:** N/A — terminal stage. However, all Closed Lost leads with a documented loss reason of "timing" or "budget cycle" are automatically flagged for Nurture re-engagement in 60–90 days.

**Loss Reason Categories (select one):**
- Price — competitor was cheaper and we could not match
- Quality — they required certifications or standards we do not hold
- Timing — they are not sourcing right now; may return
- Competitor relationship — entrenched supplier, no switching appetite currently
- Wrong fit — wrong segment, wrong volume, wrong product category
- No response — exhausted all follow-up, no engagement
- Internal — lead's company froze purchases, restructured, or went silent for internal reasons

---

### Stage 10 — Nurture

**Definition:** The lead is not ready to buy now but is worth maintaining a relationship with. They are placed on a structured re-engagement cadence.

**Entry Criteria:**
- Lead explicitly said "not now" with a future date mentioned, OR
- Lead went silent after initial engagement but was a viable profile (BANT partial score 12+), OR
- Closed Lost lead with loss reason of "timing" or "budget cycle"

**Exit Criteria:**
- Lead re-engages positively during a nurture touchpoint (reply, call request, or new inbound inquiry) — move back to MQL
- OR: lead remains unresponsive for 180 days in Nurture — archive

**Nurture Cadence:**
- Day 0: add to Nurture, log date and reason
- Day 30: touchpoint 1 — share a relevant update (new product finish, export milestone, market insight)
- Day 60: touchpoint 2 — send a case study or reference story from their segment or country
- Day 90: touchpoint 3 — direct re-engagement ("We're speaking with a buyer in your market about Verde Guatemala — wanted to check if your sourcing plans have evolved")
- Day 120: touchpoint 4 — final check-in before potential archiving
- Day 180: if still silent, archive with note

**SLA:** Every Nurture touchpoint must be sent within 3 days of its scheduled date. Touchpoints must be logged in CRM with date and content summary.

## SECTION 2 — BANT QUALIFICATION SCORECARD

### Scoring Method

Score each lead on all four BANT criteria on a scale of 0–10. Total score is out of 40.

| Score Range | Priority Level | Action |
|-------------|---------------|--------|
| 28–40 | A — Priority | Immediate sales engagement. Move to SQL. Assign senior rep. |
| 16–27 | B — Qualified | Active follow-up. Move through MQL to SQL within 14 days. |
| 8–15 | C — Nurture | Add to nurture cadence. Low-touch monthly contact. |
| 0–7 | D — Disqualify | Archive. Do not invest sales time. |

---

### B — Budget (0–10)

**Question to ask:** "What is your approximate annual spend on natural stone or marble? How many containers per year do you typically import?"

| Score | Signal |
|-------|--------|
| 0 | No budget discussion possible. Clearly a non-buyer (hobbyist, student, wrong function). |
| 2 | They buy small quantities only. No container-level purchasing. Sample curiosity only. |
| 4 | Small distributor. 1–2 containers per year. Budget exists but is modest. |
| 6 | Mid-size distributor or wholesaler. 3–6 containers per year. Regular stone buyer. Budget confirmed. |
| 8 | Large buyer. 6–12 containers per year. Dedicated stone purchasing budget. Can confirm approx. annual USD spend. |
| 10 | Tier 1 buyer. 12+ containers per year or consistent high-value annual contract. Budget authority confirmed at contact level. |

**Budget Threshold for Advancement:** Score of 6 or above required to advance from MQL to SQL.

---

### A — Authority (0–10)

**Question to ask:** "Are you the person who makes the final call on supplier selection? Who else is involved in approving a new overseas supplier?"

| Score | Signal |
|-------|--------|
| 0 | Contact is an intern, admin, or has no purchasing involvement. |
| 2 | Purchasing assistant. Has influence but cannot approve. Decision-maker is unknown. |
| 4 | Mid-level buyer or category manager. Recommends but does not approve final vendors. |
| 6 | Senior buyer or procurement manager. Strong influence. Approval from one level above required. |
| 8 | Purchasing director or VP. Signs off on suppliers. Occasional sign-off from CFO only for large contracts. |
| 10 | Owner, CEO, or Managing Director of a trading or distribution firm. Makes purchasing decisions independently. |

**Authority Threshold for Advancement:** Score of 6 or above required to advance from MQL to SQL. If score is 4, continue but also identify and contact the actual decision-maker.

---

### N — Need (0–10)

**Question to ask:** "Are you currently sourcing any green or exotic marble? Where do you source it from today? What are you trying to solve that made you interested in reaching out?"

| Score | Signal |
|-------|--------|
| 0 | No natural stone or marble need. Wrong product category entirely. |
| 2 | Occasionally sources natural stone but Verde Guatemala is not relevant to their product range. |
| 4 | Sources natural stone including some green marble, but from domestic quarries or China. Open to alternatives but not urgent. |
| 6 | Currently sources from India or competitors. Actively evaluating options. Verde Guatemala is a known product to them. |
| 8 | Has a specific client or project requiring Verde Guatemala or comparable green marble. Clear, defined need. |
| 10 | Verde Guatemala or equivalent green marble is a core SKU in their product range. Current supplier is underperforming and they need an urgent alternative. |

**Need Threshold for Advancement:** Score of 6 or above required to advance from MQL to SQL.

---

### T — Timeline (0–10)

**Question to ask:** "When are you looking to place your next stone order? Do you have any upcoming projects or client commitments driving your timeline?"

| Score | Signal |
|-------|--------|
| 0 | No timeline. No purchasing planned for the foreseeable future. |
| 2 | Vague interest. "Maybe next year." No defined project or order date. |
| 4 | Sourcing within 6–12 months. Exploring options now but no immediate urgency. |
| 6 | Sourcing within 3–6 months. Supplier evaluation is actively underway. |
| 8 | Sourcing within 1–3 months. Has a project or order window driving the decision. |
| 10 | Immediate — within 30 days. Current supplier has failed, stock is short, or project deadline is imminent. |

**Timeline Threshold for Advancement:** Score of 4 or above required to advance from MQL to SQL. A Timeline score of 2 or below should trigger Nurture, not active selling.

## SECTION 4 — DAILY CRM HYGIENE CHECKLIST

Complete every morning before beginning any outbound activity. Target completion: by 9:30 AM IST.

### Step 1 — Inbound Review (15 minutes)
- [ ] Check primary inquiry email inbox — log all new messages in CRM, assign to correct lead, set stage
- [ ] Check LinkedIn DMs and connection requests — log any sales-relevant messages
- [ ] Check WhatsApp Business — log any new buyer messages, respond to time-sensitive ones immediately
- [ ] Check any shared inquiry forms or website contact submissions (if applicable)
- [ ] All new inbound leads added to CRM with source, segment, and stage tagged

### Step 2 — SLA Breach Scan (10 minutes)
- [ ] Filter CRM for all leads with `sla_status = overdue` — review each one, take or schedule action today
- [ ] Filter CRM for all leads with `sla_status = at_risk` — confirm next action is scheduled and will be completed today
- [ ] Identify any lead that has not had a logged activity in the past 7 days — flag as stalled, define next action

### Step 3 — Stage Validation (10 minutes)
- [ ] Review all leads currently in "Contacted" — have any replied? If yes, advance to MQL
- [ ] Review all leads currently in "Proposal Sent" — have any responded? If yes, advance to Negotiation or Closed
- [ ] Review all leads currently in "Negotiation" — any pending counter-proposals to send today?
- [ ] Review all leads in "Discovery" — is any proposal overdue? Send or communicate revised timeline

### Step 4 — Task Completion Check (5 minutes)
- [ ] Open CRM task list — how many tasks are due today? Are all assigned and accepted?
- [ ] Check tasks from yesterday — were they completed? If not, reschedule with a reason logged
- [ ] Confirm today's scheduled calls are still confirmed with the leads (WhatsApp or email confirmation)

### Step 5 — Priority Actions for the Day (5 minutes)
- [ ] Write out the top 3 revenue-critical actions for today (deals closest to closing or largest opportunity value)
- [ ] Confirm time is blocked in the calendar for these 3 actions
- [ ] Set a 5:30 PM end-of-day reminder to log outcomes for today's activities in CRM before signing off
