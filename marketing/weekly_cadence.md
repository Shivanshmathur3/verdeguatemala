# Weekly Operating Cadence — The "Aggressive" Part Is a Discipline, Not a Mood

## Volume Targets (plan §2)
| Activity | Volume/week |
|---|---|
| New prospects added (researched, named contact) | 75 |
| Cold emails sent (new + sequence steps) | 250–400 |
| LinkedIn connection requests | 50–75 |
| WhatsApp outreach (Gulf) | 50 |
| Cold calls (US warm-ish) | 30–50 |
| Quotes/sample kits sent | every request, **<48h** |

## The Week (Mon–Sat)

### Monday — Prospect Day
- AM: customs-data pull → 40 new US prospects (materials, port, current supplier noted)
- PM: 20 new Gulf prospects (trade directories, LinkedIn, giga-project package lists)
- Load all into CRM + sequence tool. LinkedIn: 15 connection requests.

### Tuesday — Send Day 1
- US sequence steps fire (new Day 1s + due Day 4/9/14/21 follow-ups)
- Gulf WhatsApp block: 15 touches (Sun–Thu is the Gulf week — Tuesday is mid-week there)
- US call block: 10 calls, 7:30–9:30 PM IST. LinkedIn: 15 requests.

### Wednesday — Send Day 2 + Quotes
- Remaining sequence steps fire
- **Quote sweep: every open request answered — nothing crosses 48h**
- Gulf WhatsApp block: 15 touches. US call block: 10 calls.

### Thursday — Follow-Up Day
- Reply handling sweep: every reply gets same-day response
- Gulf WhatsApp block: 20 touches (Thursday = Gulf Friday-eve, high response)
- US call block: 10 calls. LinkedIn: 15 requests + message all new accepts.

### Friday — KPI + Hygiene
- **Fill `kpi_weekly_tracker.csv` — every column, no exceptions (plan §8)**
- Deliverability health check (bounce <3%, complaints <0.1%, blacklist scan)
- CRM hygiene: stalled deals flagged, sequences paused on repliers
- 15 remaining prospects researched to hit 75

### Saturday (half) — Content + Samples
- 3 social posts scheduled for next week (container loading, bookmatching, finish close-ups — proof-of-life)
- Sample kits packed for Monday courier. Week planned.

## The Two Tripwires (verbatim from the plan — check every Friday)

> **1. "If reply rates are under 1.5% after 3 weeks, the problem is the list or the hook, not the volume — fix message before adding volume."**

> **2. "If quote turnaround exceeds 48h in any week, stop adding volume and fix operations."**

## Funnel Math to Keep Faith With (plan §2)
1,200 prospects/quarter → 2–5% reply (24–60 conversations) → 15–25 quote/sample requests → **2–5 first containers in 90–180 days.** The buyer who samples in August orders in November. A distributor who buys one container buys 6–12/year for years.
