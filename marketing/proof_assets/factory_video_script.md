# 90-Second Factory Video — Shot List + Narration
**The plan's #1 trust asset for an unknown supplier. Shoot once on a phone, reuse forever (Day 14 email, WhatsApp Touch 3, website, LinkedIn).**

## Shot List (90 seconds total)

| Time | Shot | How to shoot |
|------|------|-------------|
| 0:00–0:08 | Owner standing in the yard, blocks behind | Phone at chest height, landscape, speak to camera |
| 0:08–0:22 | Gang-saw cutting a block, sparks/water visible | Hold steady 8s, then slow walk closer |
| 0:22–0:38 | Resin line — slabs being treated, worker inspecting | One slow pan across the line |
| 0:38–0:55 | Polishing line — slab emerging with wet shine, close-up of surface | Get the light reflecting off the polish — this is the money shot |
| 0:55–1:10 | Finished slab racks — bundle after bundle, walk-through | Walking shot, gimbal or steady hands |
| 1:10–1:25 | Container loading — crated slabs going in, straps, bracing | Show the crating detail — this answers the packing fear |
| 1:25–1:30 | Owner at the container door | Final line to camera |

## Shooting Tips
- **Golden hour** (first/last hour of daylight) in the yard; work lights are fine indoors
- Phone landscape, 1080p minimum, wipe the lens
- **No music over the voice** — machine sound + voice reads as REAL, which is the whole point
- Stabilize: two hands, elbows in, or ₹1,500 gimbal
- Overlay at the end: WhatsApp number + sales@divyastones.com
