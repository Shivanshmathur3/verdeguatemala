# Testimonial Request — to Real Past Clients
**Goal: 3–5 real, named, verifiable references to replace the "representative" testimonials on the site (plan §5.1). Make saying yes effortless.**

## The Ask (owner email)

```
Subject: [FIRST_NAME] — a two-minute favor

Hi [FIRST_NAME],

[OWNER_NAME] from Divya Stones. We shipped you [MATERIALS] over [X years
/ X containers], and I'm updating our website to show real client
references instead of anonymous ones.

Would you let me quote you? To make it effortless, here's a draft based
on our actual history — edit anything or throw it out entirely:

  "We've imported [MATERIALS] from Divya Stones since [YEAR].
   [Quality/consistency/documentation] has been reliable across
   [X] containers." — [NAME], [TITLE], [COMPANY], [CITY]

If you're comfortable, just reply "approved" (or send your version).
And if you'd rather not be named publicly, a reference I can share
privately with serious buyers is just as valuable.

Thank you either way — the business over the years mattered.

[OWNER_NAME]
Divya Stones | +91-94141-67278
```

## Follow-Up Nudge (5–7 days, WhatsApp)

```
Hi [FIRST_NAME] — [OWNER_NAME] here. Sent you a small favor request by
email (a one-line reference for our site, pre-drafted so it's zero work).
A simple "approved" is all it takes. Either way, hope business is good 🙏
```

## Rules
- Only ask clients where the relationship genuinely earned it
- Pre-draft every quote — approval is one word, writing is work
- Offer the private-reference fallback — some will help but not publicly
- Get the person's NAME and TITLE — "Stone distributor, Texas" is what we're replacing
- Store approvals (email/WhatsApp screenshots) in this folder — verifiability is the point
