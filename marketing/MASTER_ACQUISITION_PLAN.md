## 0. Hard Truths (read before executing anything)

1. **"Whichever country is importing" is not a strategy.** Two markets where timing is on our side; everything else is opportunistic inbound.
2. **The biggest asset is being wasted.** 25 years of shipments = past buyers across dozens of countries. Reactivation converts 5–10× cold. Consolidating past-buyer records into one list is Week 1's most important job — more important than any tool purchase.
3. **The website undersells a real track record.** "Representative" testimonials read as fabricated. Replace with 3–5 real, named, verifiable references + real project photos with locations. The customs trail (visible on Panjiva/ImportGenius) works FOR us — make the site match its strength.
4. **Aggressive outbound with slow follow-up is wasted money.** Quotes in 24–48h, full slab photos on demand. Can't respond same-day? Cut volume in half and fix response speed first.
5. **One bad container costs more than any campaign earns.** QC and packing discipline are part of this sales plan.

## Priority 0 — Mine the Old Network (Weeks 1–2, near-zero cost, parallel with Section 2 setup)

Most pre-COVID clients exited or stopped importing from India — so this is an **intelligence and referral play**, plus a smaller win-back on survivors. The main engine is customs-data outbound (Section 2).

1. **Build the master list**: every buyer ever — company, contact, country, materials, last order year, status (active in market / exited / unknown). 7 days.
2. **Work it three ways:**
   - **A. Still in market, not buying from India** → find out WHY (price vs China/Brazil/Turkey? freight? payment terms?). Free market research that shapes cold messaging. Then pitch post-tariff US math or new categories.
   - **B. Exited** → one warm call: "Who took over your customers? Who's buying Indian stone in your market now? Can you introduce me?" One introduction from a retired importer beats 50 cold emails. Every call ends with a referral ask.
   - **C. Still occasionally buying** → win-back with current price list + new categories. Small segment, high conversion.
3. **Channel:** personal email from the owner → WhatsApp → call. NO automation — these people know us.
4. **Expected outcome:** 3–8 warm introductions to active importers, a map of why buyers left India-sourcing, a handful of reactivated small accounts.

**Core insight:** ~21,500 international buyers purchased Indian marble in the last 12 months. The market didn't die after COVID — it changed hands. The buyers we don't know are all visible in customs data.

## 1. Market Priority Stack (July 2026 conditions)

### Priority 1 — United States (60% of effort)
**Why now:** The 50% tariff regime that crushed Indian stone imports through 2025 was struck down by the US Supreme Court Feb 20, 2026; collection ended Feb 24, refunds processing. US was ~39% of India's granite exports pre-shock. **Restocking window is open now**: importers under-ordered Indian material for 12+ months and are actively re-evaluating suppliers. The window will not stay open.

**Targets (in order):**
1. **Slab importers / stone distributors** (warehouse + resell to fabricators) — repeat container buyers. Bread and butter.
2. **Large fabricators** ($5M+ revenue) importing direct to cut distributor margin.
3. **Commercial GCs / architecture firms** — spec-driven, long cycle; cut-to-size project work only.

**Geography:** TX, FL, CA, NY, GA — ports of Houston, Miami/Port Everglades, LA/Long Beach, NY/NJ, Savannah.

**The hook:** "Tariffs are gone. Landed cost on Indian granite just dropped ~30%+. Here's an FOB/CIF quote on [material they already import] — compare it to what you paid in 2025."

### Priority 2 — Gulf: Saudi Arabia + UAE (30% of effort)
**Why:** Saudi stone demand >$6B/yr (Vision 2030: NEOM, Diriyah Gate, Red Sea Project, ROSHN; interiors sector → $18B by 2030). UAE = premium volume + re-export hub. 10–14 day shipping = fastest, cheapest lane. WhatsApp-first culture suits DM outreach.

**Targets:**
1. **Fit-out and interiors contractors** — Riyadh, Jeddah, Dubai, Abu Dhabi (per-project, big volumes).
2. **Building-material trading companies** in Dubai (re-export to Saudi/Oman/Africa).
3. **Giga-project procurement teams** — track awarded packages via AGBI's tracker and MEED; reach procurement/QS contacts.

### Priority 3 — Opportunistic (10%, inbound only)
- **Russia:** serve inbound, no outbound spend (payment/logistics friction).
- **Vietnam:** mostly raw block trade, thin margins — ignore for slab/finished business.
- **UK / Australia / Europe:** answer marketplace enquiries; push market in 2027 (Marmomac = entry event).

## 2. The Outbound Machine (build Weeks 1–2)

### Infrastructure (non-negotiable, BEFORE sending anything)
- **2–3 secondary domains** (divyastonesexport.com, divyastone.co) for cold email. NEVER cold volume from divyastones.com.
- 2–3 mailboxes/domain (Google Workspace), **warmed 2–3 weeks** (Instantly.ai or Smartlead; ~$100–150/mo).
- SPF, DKIM, DMARC on all sending domains.
- CRM: HubSpot free tier or disciplined sheet. Every prospect, every touch, logged.
- WhatsApp Business with proper catalog (products, MOQ, lead times) — Gulf channel + global follow-up channel.

### Prospect Sourcing
**#1 source: US import customs data** (Volza / ImportGenius / Panjiva, ~$150–300/mo) — every US company importing granite/marble from India (or from Brazil/Turkey/China = switchable buyers). Proven container-buyers, not guesses.

Stack: trade show exhibitor lists (Coverings, TISE/StonExpo, Marmomac, Big 5 Saudi, Middle East Stone, Xiamen) · Natural Stone Institute directory · LinkedIn Sales Navigator (~$100/mo) · Google Maps scraping ("granite distributor", "slab warehouse" in target metros).

**Volume target: 300 net-new qualified prospects/month** (200 US, 100 Gulf), each with named decision-maker, email, phone/WhatsApp.

### The Offer (first email leads with one of)
1. **Post-tariff price reset (US):** side-by-side landed-cost comparison on a material they already buy.
2. **Sample kit, freight credited** against first order.
3. **Trial LCL / mixed container:** lowers first-order risk; converts fence-sitters.

### Sequences

**US cold email (5 touches / 21 days):**
- **Day 1:** Personalized opener from customs data ("I saw you've been bringing in Steel Grey and Black Galaxy — likely via [port]"). One line on tariff removal + landed-cost comparison offer. Ask: "Worth a quote?"
- **Day 4:** Full-slab photos of 2–3 materials matching their profile + indicative FOB. Value visible IN the email.
- **Day 9:** Consistency proof — packing standard, tolerance, resin treatment, container loading photos.
- **Day 14:** 90-second facility video (shot once, reused forever). #1 trust asset for an unknown supplier.
- **Day 21:** Breakup: "Closing the file — if 2026 restocking plans change, here's my WhatsApp."

**LinkedIn (parallel):** connect (no pitch) → after accept, short tariff/landed-cost hook → move to email/WhatsApp.

**Gulf (WhatsApp-first, 4 touches):** intro + catalog PDF → project-relevant material photos (reference their giga-project/building type) → voice note/short factory video → call. **Respond to every enquiry within 2 hours during Gulf business hours.**

**Cold calls:** 10/day into warmest US prospects (opened 2×, no reply). 20-second script: who you are, tariff angle, "can I email you a quote on what you're currently importing?" Goal = permission, not close.

### Weekly Cadence
| Activity | Volume/week |
|---|---|
| New prospects added (named contact) | 75 |
| Cold emails sent (new + sequence steps) | 250–400 |
| LinkedIn connection requests | 50–75 |
| WhatsApp outreach (Gulf) | 50 |
| Cold calls (US warm-ish) | 30–50 |
| Quotes/sample kits | every request, <48h |

### Funnel Math (so nobody quits in week 6)
1,200 prospects/quarter → 2–5% reply (24–60 conversations) → 15–25 quote/sample requests → **2–5 first containers in 90–180 days**. Sample in August = order in November. One distributor = 6–12 containers/year for years. **Reply rate <1.5% after 3 weeks = fix list or hook, NOT volume.**

## 5. Proof Assets (Week 1 — package 25 years of material)
1. Replace "representative" testimonials with **3–5 real, named references**. Ask actual clients.
2. **3 one-page case studies** from real shipments: material, volume, destination, project type, photos. One US, one Gulf, one Europe/other. Cold-email attachments.
3. **90-second factory video** (gang-saw → resin line → polishing → container loading). Converts more than any brochure.
4. **Export credentials PDF**: IEC code, years exporting, countries served, shipment history summary, packing spec, LC/payment terms.
5. **Payment terms upfront:** 30% advance / 70% against BL copy standard; LC at sight for first-time large buyers.
6. **10 sample kits pre-packed**, 12 best-selling materials, courier same-day.
