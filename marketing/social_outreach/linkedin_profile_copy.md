### About Section
*(2600 characters max — copy below is approximately 2,540 characters)*

I started working in natural stone in Udaipur in 1999, when my family established Divya Stones. At the time, getting Verde Guatemala marble from quarry to an importer in the United States or Europe meant passing through two or three layers of intermediaries. Every layer added cost, added delay, and added distance between the buyer and the person who actually understood the material.

That model has not changed much across the industry. We decided early on that it was not the model we wanted to run.

Today I manage direct export relationships with stone importers and distributors across the USA, EU, and Middle East. When you work with me, you are talking to the quarry. I can tell you which lots are currently in production, what the vein character of a specific block looks like, what finish options are available for your application, and what your FOB price will be — in one conversation.

What I work on every day:

Verde Guatemala is not a commodity marble. It is a specification material. Architects and designers call it out by name for luxury hospitality, residential, and commercial interiors. That means my buyers need consistency — the same green depth, the same veining density, the same surface quality — across reorders. My job is to make that consistency possible, from block selection through finishing and packing.

We carry six varieties: Spider Green, Emerald Green, Forest Green, Imperial Green, Royal Green, and Silvo Green. Each has a distinct character. I help importers understand which variety maps to which market segment and application, so they are not guessing when they build their catalogue.

- Current lot catalogue with photography

- Flexible MOQ for first orders — we want long-term relationships, not one-time transactions

- Samples before commitment
